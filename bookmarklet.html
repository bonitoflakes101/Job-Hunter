Track Job — Bookmarklet

One click on any job page → Add Job modal opens in your JHCC dashboard with the details pre-filled.

Track This Job

← Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: View → Show Bookmarks Bar (⌘⇧B)
  2. Drag the button above into the bookmarks bar
  3. Make sure JHCC is running: npm run dev → open localhost:5173
  4. Go to any job listing page and click Track This Job in your bookmarks bar

Supported Sites

LinkedIn JobStreet Indeed AWS Jobs Any site (fallback)

What it extracts: job title, company name, page URL, and source site.
What it does not do: submit anything, read passwords, or access any data outside the job listing.
Requires: JHCC running at localhost:5173.
